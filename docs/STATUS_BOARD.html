The bottom line

The vertical slice is feature-complete and the Unity/FishNet port is substantially done. Every rule in GAME_DESIGN.md is implemented, and every automated gate is green: csharp/Parity reports PARITY OK, 39/39 vitest, client and server tsc --noEmit clean, and a headless SetUpScene compiles with 0 error CS and 0 shader errors. Re‑theme: Hollow Pines → Metoh

1 Aug · Shipped

The project shipped as Hollow Pines, a Pacific‑NW Bigfoot hunt, which overlapped the Steam title BIGFOOT. Rebranded on branch yeti_port: namespaces, directories, the Colyseus room, the Unity scene, packaging, and the audio synthesis retuned from wood/water to ice/wind.

The web build keeps its forest visuals on purpose — only identifiers were renamed there. Web visuals are abandoned; the Unity build carries the snow re-theme.

Parity impact Sim key names changed, so golden.json was regenerated and the parity CI job added the same week to catch TS↔C# drift on every push.

Deep snow & trails — two seed-derived zones

1 Aug · Needs balancing

Two separate derived zones, spec in GAME_DESIGN.md §7.7. Slow: only low ground below PLAYER.driftHeight holds drift; searchers fall toward deepSnowFactor, the Yeti is unaffected — ~⅓ of the map, so it is a routing choice rather than a flat nerf. Prints: anywhere off-trail (the wider rule), a moving searcher drops a snowprint clue replicated to all but render-filtered to the Yeti only.

Both live in stepPlayer, so the slow is applied by whoever simulates the player on both builds; applyMove validates, it does not re-simulate. Zones derive from the seed and are never replicated (deepSnowDepth/leavesSnowPrints in shared/sim/movement.ts plus the C# mirror).

Open deepSnowFactor, driftHeight, driftDepth and print stride/lifetime are all first-guess values. Unplayed since it landed.

Both CPU brains replaced with belief + utility

14 Aug · Shipped

Play-test report: “searchers beeline for the yeti … it’s like an AI game from the 80s, basic if-else.” Accurate, and the cause was not the number of states — a pursuit function over perfect information always produces a straight line, and both brains had one. Three omniscience leaks, all closed: clue scoring weighted freshness ~66× over distance (so it homed on a live position); FreshestTrailClue had no distance gate at all, so a searcher 700 m away “saw” a footprint; and YetiBot’s Mode.Hunt fallback walked at NearestSearcherRaw() through walls.

New Scripts/Game/AI/: BeliefMap (32×32 occupancy filter — sightings collapse it, rumours blur it, negative information stops re-checking cleared ground, and diffusion turns a stale sighting into a growing search area), BotPerception, TeamBlackboard (belief is private, intent is shared — pooling belief would give four bodies one pair of eyes), UtilityAction with commitment + dwell replacing both fixed ladders, and BotProfile weights derived from the Specialties table so retuning FilmRangeMul moves Eli’s behaviour automatically.

New behaviours: RECOVER (ServerBotRecoverPile was wired and never called), OVERWATCH, and GUARD/AMBUSH/STALK plus a pressure governor that eases off ~18 s after a grab. Difficulty tiers landed as belief quality (BotDifficulty), so the same behaviours run at every tier and you cannot read difficulty off how a bot moves.

Why four searchers looked like one CampSpot() returns an 8×4 m box and every bot was placed by it — four bots stacked, then running identical deterministic decisions, never separated. Now fanned on a ring at golden-angle bearings. Torch state also gates evidence sight at UnlitSightFactor, which is what finally makes the torch a sensor rather than a status icon.

Three graphics passes — camp/trails, tower/trees/caves, pipeline gaps

14–15 Aug · Shipped

[camp-gfx] — campfire was one static emissive cone plus a constant light; now logs, ember bed, flame/spark/smoke particles and flicker from summed sines rather than Random (random flicker is frame-rate dependent and reads as a failing bulb). Trails read as decals for three reasons, all fixed: a flat ribbon floating 4 cm up, sharing SnowNormal with virgin powder, and perfect uniformity along their length — now a trodden channel with berms, two submeshes and a new PackedSnowNormal.

[tower-trees-caves] — ladder stiles were TaperedCylinder(…, 4), i.e. square; the tower had no cross-bracing (the cheapest silhouette win at distance); the tower lamp had no mesh at all. Tree sway shipped as Metoh/TreeSway + Sway.hlsl — it had to be a vertex shader because trees are merged per chunk via CombineInstance, leaving no per-tree transform. Cave mouths were convex twice over, so MeshUtil.Throat now builds inward-facing faces with reversed windings.

[pipeline-gaps] — the build had no anti-aliasing at all: MSAA off in HPQuality and nothing put in its place, so since render scale went to native 1.0 every edge has been a raw step. Now SMAA (high) on the expensive tier, FXAA on the cheap one, re-applied from HPQuality.ApplyRenderScale so the pause-menu slider cannot strip it. Plus soft particles in Weather (billboards were being sliced into white polygons at every intersection) and a procedural ProcTex.TorchCookie replacing a perfect mathematical disc.

The pattern worth naming Three of the four wins in [pipeline-gaps] were settings never switched on. Metoh → Configure Render Pipeline had existed for two weeks and never been run — both RP assets still read m_ColorGradingMode: 0 (LDR), and the SSAO on PC_Renderer was the URP template’s own. Third pass running where the biggest gain was an unclaimed switch, after [legibility]’s render scale and [snow-glitter]’s HDR. Audit the switches before the art.

Line-by-line audit of the Unity build

15 Aug · 15 fixed, 30 open

unity/Assets/Metoh/, the four shaders and csharp/Metoh.Sim read against the docs, then a headless rebuild to prove the fixes. Fourteen critical/high fixed same-day, including: Weather parented its emitters to the WorldBuilder transform, which Rebuild() destroys — the host’s seed roll killed snow on the first match of every session; TreeSway.shader declared _BumpMap and never sampled it, and was missing _FORWARD_PLUS, so no light reached a trunk; DropSnowPrints passed raw Unity yaw where ClueMarker expects sim yaw, laying every print 180° out; and TargetTeleport rebuilt the local sim with a full battery while ServerVitals refuses a rise, desyncing torch state from night 2.

Thirty items remain, ranked with file references in Aug_15_Bug.md. Highest-value open ones: Metoh.Sim.Collision walks all ~2,400 colliders with no broad phase; ~200 short-lived collections/sec in the 20 Hz authoritative loop; and 38 new GUIStyle per OnGUI, several inside per-item loops.

The last open report closed — and one toolchain trap left “The first loading screen is always broken” was async shader compilation, not an art or asset fault: with the editor default on, it renders placeholders rather than waiting for variants, which is why it self-heals on a delay and fires after every scene rebuild. Fixed by RenderPipelineSetup.DisableAsyncShaderCompilation, asserted from both Configure and SetUpScene — the latter being the command that causes the reimport. Still open: a headless SetUpScene was observed once to compile clean and exit without invoking the method, so verification must require the Mountain scene wired line, not just a zero error count.

How we got here

Eight weeks, one pivot of premise and one pivot of technology

  1. 19 June 2026
    Three.js + Colyseus scaffold, runnable standalone

    Vite/TypeScript client, low-poly terrain with instanced conifers, first-person controller, MountainRoom + GameState schema. GAME_DESIGN.md written the same week and the source of truth since.

  2. Late June – early July
    Phases 1–5: the full match loop, then polish

    Clue trail with lifetime decay, roar/grab/incapacitate, filming as the win condition, three-night structure with per-night ESCALATION, then searcher counterplay (revive, dazzle, vault) and the polish phase — EffectComposer post, settings, key rebinding, dusk briefing.

  3. 11 July
    Server authority + the specialties layer

    Movement moved server-authoritative with client reconciliation (applyMove: bounds clamp, speed-gate token bucket, collision pushout) and antiCheat.ts recomputes filming LOS server-side. Five scalar specialties shipped over a persona-assignment layer.

  4. 12 – 19 July
    Engine pivot — Unity + FishNet, sequenced netcode-first

    Target changed from a hosted web deploy to a downloadable Steam build with Steam Datagram Relay. R1 relay slice scaffolded; R3 ported shared/sim to csharp/Metoh.Sim with the csharp/Parity harness verifying it against the TS sim. Unity migration alpha 1.0 on 19 July.

  5. 1 – 6 August
    Five days committed without Unity compiling them once

    unity/Assets/Metoh/ in this repo is source only — not an openable project. The editor opens C:\Users\amedi\Metoh_port, and code moves between them only by explicit robocopy. Two passes were pushed against the wrong tree. Play-test reports in that window were accurate; the wrong assumption was ours. Symptom: the terrain shader had never once parsed.

  6. 14 – 15 August
    AI rewrite, three graphics passes, full audit

    Committed as 9cd85dc and synced to Metoh_port. All gates green; UNITY_NOTES.md gained [camp-gfx], [tower-trees-caves] and [pipeline-gaps]. Zero frames run.

What this asks for

The three decisions that move the schedule

First

A Play-mode session on Metoh_port

Nothing else here can be ranked first. It unblocks the balance pass, confirms the async-shader fix actually cleared the bad first load (watch the [boot] line and any STARTUP PROBLEMS block from BootReport), and reads the LogBotSummary histogram to say whether the utility weights are close. The AI soak harness is deliberately deferred until after this.

Second

A call on rigged character assets

The only Phase 6 gap engineering cannot close alone. ICharacterBody /CharacterFactory is finished, so this is purely a sourcing decision: buy, contract, or ship procedural Avatar.cs bodies for a first release. Procedural stays the default and the fallback either way.

Third

Target release shape

Steam/SDR integration is the last unstarted block (Phase 8, R2/R4–R6) and its size depends on the target: closed playtest, early access, or full launch. Each implies a different amount of Phase 7 — the accessibility audit and the balancing pass.
